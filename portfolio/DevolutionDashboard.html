Greater Manchester devolution dashboard

CC6 | Greater Manchester compared with England

← Back to the coding challenges

This dashboard examines the development of Greater Manchester since the start of its devolution efforts in 2014. It compares the trends of key indicators to the English average The charts are based on data from the ONS Nomis API. Six time series were batch downloaded with a loop. The corresponding code is publicly available in this Google Colab Notebook

Source: ONS, Nomis (Population Estimates, Annual Population Survey, UK Business Counts, Annual Survey of Hours and Earnings). Dashed lines: devolution deal (November 2014) and first elected mayor (May 2017).
